NumberField

A text box for numbers with a spinner inside its border. It shares the label and validation options of every field — FieldLabel, LabelAlign, LabelWidth, EmptyText, ReadOnly, AllowBlank, MaxLength, MinLength, EnforceMaxLength — and adds Editable, MinValue, MaxValue and HideTrigger.

Spinner

The arrows add and subtract 1. While the field is empty the up arrow writes 1 and the down arrow -1.

LabelAlign

Width, LabelWidth and Height

Margin, MarginSpec, Padding and PaddingSpec

ReadOnly, Editable and HideTrigger

ReadOnly hides the spinner and locks the keyboard, Editable="false" locks only the keyboard, and HideTrigger hides only the spinner.

Hidden

MinValue, MaxValue and isValid()

Valid between 1 and 10. The spinner never steps past those limits, but a value out of range can still be typed: it only makes the field invalid.
